About the Author

Nqaba Nhantsi

MACRO & QUANTITATIVE TRADER · CEO, NHANTSI CAPITAL TRADING

Nqaba Nhantsi is a macro and quantitative trader, trading educator, and CEO of Nhantsi Capital Trading, who developed Nqaba Fractal Theory, Nqaba Fractal Time Zones, the Nqaba Asian Model, and the Institutional Values Model after years of studying how institutional participants actually build positions in gold, indices, and major currency pairs.
